# Lumina

**Lumina** is a semantic navigation game where players traverse a network of evocative concepts to bridge the gap between two distant ideas.

## Concept

In a world of scattered meanings, your goal is to find the thread that connects "Chaos" to "Order", "Silence" to "Thunder", or "Ephemeral" to "Eternal".

## How to Play

1.  **Select a Level:** Choose a conceptual bridge to build (e.g., Chaos -> Order).
2.  **Navigate:** You start at the first concept. Select one of the related words (neighbors) to move to that concept.
3.  **Bridge the Gap:** Continue selecting words until you reach the target concept.
4.  **Optimize:** Try to reach the target in as few steps as possible (Par).

## Features

- **Intelligent Graph:** A curated network of high-brow concepts and definitions.
- **Visuals:** Dark, moody, glassmorphic aesthetic with smooth transitions.
- **Social:** Share your path and score with friends.
